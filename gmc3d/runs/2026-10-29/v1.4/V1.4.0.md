# v1.4.0 results (29 Oct 2026): tilted shapes, white and periodic walls

Everything below comes from `validate.log` and `tilted_seeds.log` in this
folder. 2M histories per validate run; OpenMC 0.16 in 400 batches; pass
rule as before (no tally chi2 with p < 0.001, no single value with
|z| > 3.29).

## What is new

- `Cylinder` and `Cone` along any axis (OpenMC's general forms).
- White walls (a particle comes back with a cosine-law direction).
- Translational periodic walls (leave through one plane, come back
  through its partner with the same direction).

## The new test problem: `tilted`

A 20 x 20 x 30 cm block (scatterer) with an absorbing pipe (r = 3 cm)
along (1, 1, 1) and a narrow scattering cone along (1, -1, 2). x walls
periodic, y walls white, z faces vacuum, so one history meets all three
kinds of wall.

Both PASS. Plain MC's absorption at z -2.39 and its face currents at
3.30 (chi2 6.6 on 2 bins, p 0.037) were the largest numbers, so plain MC

(`tilted_seeds.log`): absorption z +0.16 and -0.30, face currents 1.25 and
1.26, mesh 0.99 and 0.96. Noise.

## The other six problems

Bit-identical to v1.3 at the same seed (the log differs only in timings).
The two known borderline items are still there with the same numbers:
slab plain MC depth mesh chi2/dof 1.766 and nested balls leakage z -3.36,
both shown to be noise in v1.2 (five more seeds; 10M histories). So
validate's overall line still reads FAIL for those two, as in v1.2 and
v1.3.

## Unit tests (108 pass, 16 new in `tests/test_geometry_v140.py`)

- The cylinder and cone functions are positive multiples of OpenMC's own
  (same surface, same sides) on 2,000 points.
- Flights: on 500 random rays no crossing is skipped, and the end point
  is on the surface.
- Nearest distance: on 300 points never larger than the distance to a
  fine grid of surface points, and at most 0.008 cm smaller (grid
  spacing). The ball check that no ball crosses a surface also passes on
  200,000 points of the tilted problem.
- White wall: mu^2 and the azimuth uniform over 50,000 draws; a closed
  white sphere absorbs every particle.
- Periodic walls in the uniform slab give the same leakage as mirror
  walls.
- Refusals: rotational periodic, albedo, current on a white surface.

## Limits

- Rotational periodic walls (partner planes not parallel) are refused;
  that is v1.4.1.
- A periodic wall on a curved surface is refused (OpenMC refuses it too).
- General `Plane`s pair only through `periodic_surface`; X/Y/Z planes
  pair automatically, as in OpenMC.
- `albedo` other than 1 and current tallies on white or periodic walls
  are refused.
