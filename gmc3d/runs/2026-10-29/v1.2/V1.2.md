# v1.2.0 results (29 Oct 2026): sources

Everything below comes from `sourcecheck.log`, `validate.log` and
`recheck.log` in this folder. 2M histories per run unless stated; OpenMC
0.16 in 400 batches. Pass rule: no tally whose chi2 has p < 0.001, and no
single number with |z| > 3.29.

## Validate (`python run.py validate`): the old problems on the new source path

Four problems pass outright. Two failed on the first run:

| problem | what failed | first run | rechecked |
|---|---|---|---|
| slab, plain MC | depth mesh chi2/dof over 60 bins | 1.766 (p 2.4e-4) | seeds 6-10: 0.85, 0.53, 0.71, 0.70, 0.41, all PASS |
| nested, exact balls | leakage z | -3.36 | 10M histories, plain MC: leakage z +0.79, flux z +1.12 |

**Slab.** The 60 depth bins share histories, so they rise and fall
together. chi2 assumes independent bins and swings wider than its table
says (0.41 to 1.77 over six seeds). This is the same thing seen in v1.1.

**Nested.** At 2M histories plain MC and balls both sat low against
OpenMC on all ten seed/reference pairs tried (flux z -0.7 to -3.2). At 10M
on both sides the gap is gone. The 2M OpenMC references themselves differ
by 0.1% (flux 17.486 with seed 101, 17.504 with seed 105, 17.460 at 10M).
This closes the "nested leakage z -3.2" open check in `GAPS.md`, which
had been open since v1.1.

Note: the rechecks reran the cached slab and nested OpenMC references with
seed 105, so the next `validate` reruns them (about 20 s each).

## Found along the way (OpenMC 0.16, measured)

- **Tallies are per source particle; leakage is per unit of source
  weight.** Each site's weight is the sum of all source strengths, so
  `strength = 3` multiplies every tally by 3 and leaves leakage unchanged.
  Before v1.2 we ignored strengths: a silent wrong answer for any source
  with strength other than 1.
- `rejection_strategy = "kill"` behaves like `resample` for domain
  constraints; `energy_bounds` and `time_bounds` do nothing in a
  one-group run. Both are now warned, not ignored silently.
- OpenMC stops if fewer than 5% of sites meet the constraints. We accept
  any fraction (deepcell: about 11 sites drawn per site kept).
- The XML OpenMC's own Python writes for a `PointCloud` bias is refused by
  its C++. Our PointCloud bias is checked by unit test only.

Unit tests: 20 new, 82 in total, all pass.
