## Found along the way

- OpenMC 0.16 crashes (segmentation fault) when a multigroup track-length
  `scatter` tally is scored in a void cell. The first validate run died on
  the cask that way. The cask now scores scatter on a material tally.
- `MeshSurfaceFilter` and `MeshBornFilter` are subclasses of OpenMC's
  `MeshFilter`, so before v1.1.3 they were accepted and scored as ordinary
  meshes: a silent wrong answer. Now refused.
- `maps.json` holds only the cask: the first maps run stopped on the same
  OpenMC crash after the slab (the slab's numbers are in `maps.log`).
