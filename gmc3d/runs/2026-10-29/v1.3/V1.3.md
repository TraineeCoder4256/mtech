# v1.3.0 results (29 Oct 2026): tallies with several filters

Everything below comes from `validate.log`, `partial_probe.log` and
`speed.log` in this folder. 2M histories per validate run; OpenMC 0.16 in
400 batches; pass rule as before (no tally chi2 with p < 0.001, no single
value with |z| > 3.29).

## What a tally can now be

Any mix of Cell, Material, Mesh and Energy filters on a track tally, and
Surface with Cell, CellFrom, Material and Energy on a current tally. The
combined bin is OpenMC's (last filter fastest), so results line up with
OpenMC's `tally.mean` row for row. A cell filter sees the cell at every
level of the particle's path, so a cell filled with a lattice or a
universe scores whatever happens inside it.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| nested | filled (lattice cell, universe cell, a pin 3 levels down, reflector) | 4 | 1.125 | 2.053 |
| nested | mesh x material, all 4 scores | 96 hit | 0.874 | 0.933 |

| nested | partial currents: from core / reflector x core face / shell face | 3 hit | 0.866 | 0.370 |

The two-bin cell x energy at 3.72 is chi2 7.4 on 2 bins (p 0.025): it is
the same core / reflector flux as the `filled` tally, one number per cell,
so it moves with it. Everything else validate checks is bit-identical to
v1.2 at the same seed, including the two known borderline items there:
slab plain MC depth mesh 1.766 and nested balls leakage z -3.36. Both were
shown to be noise in v1.2 (five more seeds; 10M histories).

## Bug found: current through an inner surface (since v1.1)

A `SurfaceFilter` on a transmission surface counted every flight crossing
the surface's whole plane, including where it runs outside the cell's real
boundary (the plane x = 4 beyond the core's face, with reflector on both
sides). OpenMC counts only real crossings. On the nested core face, net
current: ours 0.0821, OpenMC 0.0408. Fixed: a crossing that stays in the
same root cell is not counted; now 0.0415 vs 0.0408. No test problem used
an inner surface before v1.3, so no earlier result changes (all the
validated current tallies were on vacuum faces).

## OpenMC conventions measured (OpenMC 0.16)

- At a crossing, a cell or material filter sees the cell ENTERED and a
  cell-from filter the cell LEFT; on a vacuum face both see the cell left.
- One energy group [0, 20 MeV]: every particle sits at the midpoint,
  10 MeV, whatever the source energy.

## Speed: plain MC is 5-16% slower than v1.2

Same container, back to back, 500k histories, best of 3 (`speed.log`):
sphere 3.89 s (v1.2) vs 4.10 s (v1.3), cask 11.64 s vs 13.50 s. The first
v1.3 build was 31-45% slower; most of that was one line (a constant
negative array index that made Numba's compiled code about 10x slower)
and is fixed. Tallies with one cell or material filter take a shortcut
past the general bin-combining code. What is left is not found yet. With no
tallies at all the cask runs as fast as v1.2 (4.96 vs 5.01 s) and the
sphere 14% slower (2.62 vs 2.29 s; the descent now records the cell at
every level). With tallies, the cask's cell / material tallies cost 10%
more in all and its mesh tallies 13% more.

Unit tests: 10 new, 92 in total, all pass.
